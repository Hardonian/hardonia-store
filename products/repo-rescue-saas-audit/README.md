# SaaS Repo Rescue Audit

A fixed-scope technical audit for production SaaS teams whose revenue and trust depend on working checkout, authentication, authorization, webhooks, fulfillment, and deployment paths.

## Outcome
You receive an evidence-backed risk register and a remediation sequence that identifies the highest-risk buyer-path failures first. Findings distinguish verified defects, risk hypotheses, and items requiring access or product decisions.

## Scope
- repository and runtime inventory
- auth and tenant-isolation review
- billing, checkout, webhook, and fulfillment path review
- release, health, and buyer-journey verification
- prioritized remediation plan with rollback notes

## Price and delivery
The audit is $499 fixed scope. Delivery target is two business days after access and scope confirmation. A $1,500 fix sprint is optional and is proposed only after the audit findings are reviewed.

## Safety boundary
No deployment, billing change, production write, outreach, or destructive action is performed without explicit approval. Secrets are not copied into the report.
