# Data Retention Policy

## Retention Periods
- Financial records: 7 years (CRA requirement)
- Usage logs: 2 years
- Webhook events: 1 year
- Support tickets: 3 years
- Delivery tokens: 30 days or until redeemed
- Analytics aggregates: indefinite

## Deletion
User deletion requests remove personal identifiers but preserve anonymized financial records where required by law.

## Backups
Daily automated backups retained for 30 days. Off-site backup recommended for production.
