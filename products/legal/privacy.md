# Privacy Policy

## 1. Data Controller
Scott Hardie, Ontario, Canada. Email: privacy@hardonia.ca

## 2. Data Collected
- Email address (for delivery, support, receipts)
- Payment info (processed by Stripe, not stored locally)
- Usage metrics (API calls, tokens, job counts)
- IP addresses and user agents (security/audit logs)

## 3. Data Retention
- Purchase records: 7 years (Canadian tax law)
- Usage logs: 2 years
- Delivery tokens: expire 30 days after issue
- Support tickets: 3 years

## 4. Data Sharing
Data is not sold. Third-party processors: Stripe (payments), GitHub (code hosting), Vercel/Caddy (hosting).

## 5. Cookies
Session cookies only. No advertising cookies. Analytics cookies can be disabled.

## 6. Your Rights
You may request data export or deletion by emailing privacy@hardonia.ca. Response within 30 days.

## 7. Security
Data encrypted in transit (TLS). Local DB files are not encrypted at rest; plan for encryption before production scale.

## 8. International Transfers
Data may be processed in Canada and the United States. By using services, you consent to cross-border transfers.

## 9. Changes
Privacy Policy may be updated. Material changes will be communicated via email or service notice.
