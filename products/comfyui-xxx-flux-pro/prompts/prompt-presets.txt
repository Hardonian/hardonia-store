# FLUX Fantasy PRO — 30 prompt presets (fictional, consensual, 18+)

Usage: pass as `--prompt "<preset text>"` to scripts/generate_flux.py in this
pack (set --steps 20-24 for FLUX), or paste into the positive text widget in
the UI workflow. Presets are grouped; base presets 1-6 are shared with the
FLUX Fantasy pack, 7-30 are PRO-only.
