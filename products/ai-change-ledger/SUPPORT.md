# Support

## Verification fails

Restore the ledger from the last known-good backup. Do not rewrite historical receipts in place.

## A lane is unreachable

The receipt records the lane as unreachable; this is evidence, not a silent success. Check the local Ollama service and rerun.

## Scope

The product is an evidence layer. It does not replace backups, monitoring, access control, incident response, or human approval.
