# ComfyUI Product Photo Kit

Clean, consistent product photography workflows — without a studio.

## What's included
- The ComfyUI product-photo workflow (nodes + graph)
- Supporting assets (lighting/backdrop templates, sample inputs)
- Setup walkthrough (README)
- Local validation steps

## Setup
1. Review README.md
2. Import the workflow into ComfyUI
3. Run local validation before sharing or deploying

## Use it for
- E-commerce product shots
- Catalog imagery
- On-brand batch product photos

Runs locally. Iterate on lighting and backdrop until it's on-brand.
