# Private AI Assurance Retainer — founding validation packet

## Buyer promise

Every month, receive evidence-backed answers to:

- Did our model, prompt, tool, or dependency change?
- Can the authorized agent still access only what it should?
- Did reliability, latency, or routing regress?
- Can we prove our privacy and operational controls?
- What should we fix next?

## Delivery boundary

Local-first by default. Synthetic or customer-approved test data only. No third-party scanning. No production actions without explicit authorization.

## First-month deliverables

1. Asset and scope inventory
2. Baseline assurance report
3. Test corpus and regression configuration
4. Ranked remediation queue
5. Evidence archive and executive summary

## Conversion gate

The offer remains early-access until a human approves the final recurring Stripe price, contract language, intake form, and customer-specific authorization process.
