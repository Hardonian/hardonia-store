# Private AI Assurance Retainer

A recurring evidence and operations service for small AI SaaS teams, agencies, and privacy-sensitive operators.

## Outcome
You receive a repeatable monthly assurance cycle: local evidence collection, regression checks, security findings, remediation priorities, and a proof report. This is an operational assurance service, not certification or a guarantee of perfect model behavior.

## Included
- agreed system baseline and scope
- evidence collection against the agreed environment
- regression and health checks
- prioritized findings with severity and owner
- monthly report and operator review

## Not included
Legal advice, formal SOC 2/HIPAA/GDPR certification, unlimited incident response, or changes to customer systems without approval.
