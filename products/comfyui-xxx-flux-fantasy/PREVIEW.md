**ComfyUI FLUX Fantasy — sample output descriptions (no imagery in this doc)**

What this pack produces (validated renders, 832×832 and 1024×1024):

| Preset | Look |
|---|---|
| `neon-sovereign` | glowing cyan geometric shield sigil, clean vector stroke, high contrast |
| `cinematic-gaurdian` | dramatic low-key portrait, rim light, film grain, editorial grade |
| `low-light-editorial` | studio portrait, shallow depth of field, warm shadows |
| `abstract-vector` | iridescent gradient vector burst, crisp geometry |

These presets are the same ones exercised in the WALKTHROUGH. Actual output size
is controlled by `--width/--height` (combinations from 640×640 to 1024×1216
verified in ~11 GB VRAM).

Renders are 100% local on your GPU — no cloud, no watermarks, no API keys.
