# FLUX Fantasy — prompt presets (fictional, consensual, 18+)

All presets target fictional characters in consensual adult-fantasy scenarios.
Do not use with real persons, minors, or non-consensual content (see
LEGAL_SAFEGUARDS.md).

1. neon-sovereign: neon sovereign shield sigil, clean vector stroke, glowing cyan edges, geometric frame
2. cinematic-guardian: cinematic portrait of an adult fantasy guardian, neon crown, volumetric rim light, editorial grade
3. low-light-editorial: studio portrait, dramatic low-key lighting, fine grain, shallow depth
4. abstract-vector: abstract vector burst, iridescent gradients, sharp geometry, high contrast
5. fantasy-royalty: fictional royal consort portrait, ornate fabric, candlelight, painterly finish
6. noir-nocturne: noir nightclub fantasy scene, single-source practical light, smoke, film grain

Usage: append as --prompt "<preset text>" to scripts/generate_flux.py, or paste
into the CLIPTextEncode text widget in the UI workflow.
