# Brand & Voice — Hardonia

## Who we are
Hardonia builds and ships local-first AI infrastructure products: private inference access,
compute API keys, ComfyUI workflow packs, n8n automation kits, and ops playbooks. Everything is
digital, downloadable, and privacy-respecting by design. We do not train on customer data.

## Voice
- Direct. No fluff, no hype, no "AI slop."
- Honest about limitations (e.g. "this runs on your hardware," "subscription renews monthly").
- Technical but plain — assume the reader is competent but busy.
- Never promise what the product can't deliver.

## Do
- Link to the exact product folder / Gumroad listing.
- Give the customer the next concrete step.
- Route billing questions to Gumroad (we don't hold card data).
- Use real status (Cloudflare tunnel, local-first) in infra explanations.

## Don't
- Don't invent product features, SLAs, or guarantee uptime numbers we don't have.
- Don't expose internal infra details (IPs, tokens, server topology) to customers.
- Don't promise "24/7" unless we staff it. Current support response: 2-3 business days.
- Don't tell customers to edit Caddyfiles or SSH into anything. That's internal.
