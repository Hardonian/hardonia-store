## Ongoing — operating rules
- Single source of truth is this KB. If you learn something new, update the right doc, not a DM.
- The AU bot pulls from `faq-customer.md` + `runbooks/`. Keep those current or the bot lies.
- Never expose internal infra (IPs, tokens, server topology) to customers or public docs.
- Financial/compute/legal context lives in `kb/feeds/snapshot.json` (regenerated by `tools/ecosystem-update.sh`).
  Read it before answering "are we at capacity?" or "what's our margin?" type questions.

## First task suggestion
Pick one thin SUPPORT.md in `products/*/` and expand it using `templates/reply-customer.md` patterns,
then propose the FAQ entry. This builds the muscle and de-risks scale.
